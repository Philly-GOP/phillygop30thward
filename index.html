Who we are

17 divisions. 34 seats. One ward leader.

Every division in the 30th gets two Republican committee people, and Scott leads them all. Call it our division of labor. The 30th Ward Republican Committee organizes Republicans in Graduate Hospital and Southwest Center City for the Grand Old Party, block by block. For the citywide party, visit the Philadelphia Republican Party (Philly GOP).

Ward Leader

Scott Cohen

Scott leads the 30th Ward Republican Committee. His focus is simple: a committee person in every division, and a Republican at every polling place in the ward on Election Day.

Committeeperson · Division 4

Shirah Brown

Shirah represents Division 4 and is Scott's right hand (pun intended). She runs the ward's operations and communications, and is happy to walk you through what a seat involves.

    Get In Touch

    Questions? Want to help? Talk to us.

    Learn more about the ward, find a way to get involved, or just ask a question. Our ward reads every message, and you will hear back from a neighbor, not a form letter.

    Prefer email? Write to info@phillygop30thward.com.

    I would like to

    Live outside the 30th Ward? We will pass your note to your own ward's Republican leader so the right neighbor gets back to you. We use this only to get back to you. We do not sell it and we do not add you to anything you did not ask for.

    Report a Block Issue

    Something on your block that needs attention?

    A broken streetlight, a problem property, trash that never gets picked up. Tell us what is going on and we will look into it. If you would like, we will bring it to our city or state representatives.

    For emergencies, call 911. For routine city services, you can also use Philly311.

    What kind of issue

    A neighbor from the ward reads every report. We use your details only to follow up on this issue. We do not sell them and we do not add you to anything you did not ask for.
