How your ward works.

Philadelphia politics starts on your block. Here is what a ward is, what committee people and ward leaders do, and how a neighbor takes a seat.

The basics

Wards, divisions and seats.

The ward

Philadelphia is organized into 66 political wards. The 30th covers Graduate Hospital and Southwest Center City.

The division

Each ward is split into divisions, usually just a few blocks and one polling place. The 30th has 17.

The committee seats

Each division elects up to two Republican committee people. Together, they form the ward's Republican committee.

The roles

Who does what.

Being a committeeperson

A committeeperson is the Republican Party's point of contact for a division.

  • Knows the neighbors in the division
  • Works the polls in the primary and the general
  • Votes to elect the ward leader

What a committee seat involves

Running a ward

The ward leader is elected by the ward's committee people soon after they are elected.

  • Leads the ward committee
  • Represents the ward on the Republican City Committee
  • Fills open committee seats

Find your ward and its leader

Taking a seat

How a neighbor becomes a committeeperson.

Who qualifies

You must be a registered Republican and live in the division you represent.

By election

Committee people serve four-year terms and are elected in the spring primary. The most recent election was May 2026.

By appointment

Between elections, an open seat can be filled by the ward leader. You do not have to wait for the next primary.
